# Amazon-products-Sales-Analysis-PowerBI-project
## Project Summary
This project delivers an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze a comprehensive e-commerce dataset spanning some major categories such as: Audio Video, Camera, Car Accessories, etc. By transforming raw data into a dynamic visual experience, the dashboard provides actionable insights into product pricing strategies, customer review volumes, order trends over time, and shipment distributions.

The project focuses on answering key business questions, such as:
- Which product categories and specific items drive the most customer engagement?
- How are products distributed across different price bands?
- What are the seasonal and yearly order volume trends?
- How does review volume correlate with product pricing?

Through the use of Power Query for robust data cleaning, a custom Date Table for accurate time-intelligence reporting, and DAX measures, this dashboard allows users to seamlessly explore and uncover the underlying patterns in Amazon's product listings.

---
## Problem Statement
The goal of this project is to analyze the Amazon product landscape and provide meaningful insights into sales performance and customer engagement using key performance indicators (KPIs) such as:
**YTD Sales:**
Understand the overall revenue generated year-to-date across all product categories.
**QTD Sales:**
Measure the current quarter's revenue to evaluate short-term business performance and momentum.
**YTD Products Sold:**
Analyze the total volume of individual items sold year-to-date to gauge market demand.
**YTD Reviews:**
Measure the total customer engagement and feedback volume to assess customer satisfaction and product popularity.

## Charts and Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

**Sales by Month**
- Chart Type: Area Chart
- Insight: Visualizes sales trends over time on a monthly basis to identify seasonal patterns and overall growth trends (e.g., the notable spike in September and November/December).

**Sales by Week**
- Chart Type: Column Chart
- Insight: Displays sales data on a weekly basis to pinpoint shorter-term fluctuations and granular performance insights, highlighting a significant surge in the later weeks of the year (weeks 40-50).

**Sales by Product Category**
- Chart Type: Table/Matrix
- Insight: Provides a high-level overview of revenue distribution, comparing YTD Sales, QTD Sales, and the percentage of total YTD sales across categories. It highlights that Men Shoes and Camera are the top revenue drivers.

**Top 5 Products by YTD Sales**
- Chart Type: Horizontal Bar Chart
- Insight: Highlights the top-performing, revenue-generating products (e.g., Nikon, Atomos), allowing focus on the key financial drivers.

**Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews**
- Chart Type: Horizontal Bar Chart
- Insight: Identifies the most popular products based on customer review volume (e.g., SanDisk, JETech), revealing consumer preferences and high-engagement items.
